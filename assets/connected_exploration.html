Source, method and limits

Copernicus DEM GLO-30 Public · approximately 30 m native sampling · nearest native cell. Profile samples follow the WGS 84 ellipsoidal geodesic at intervals no greater than 30 m; extra samples do not increase source resolution. Missing values appear as gaps.

Horizontal offsets: WGS 84 / UTM zone 23S (EPSG:32723), in meters. Surface elevations: EGM2008, in meters. The 3D scene uses equal spatial scaling with no vertical exaggeration. The profile chart uses independent horizontal and vertical axes. Vegetation and buildings may influence the DSM. Values over water are modeled elevations, not depths, surveyed water levels or ground truth. This is not a walking route.
