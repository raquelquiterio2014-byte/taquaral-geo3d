Review process and scope

Each included cell was visually checked against the rendered PDF, including date/station columns and units. Decimal commas remain available in Printed value; normalized values use decimal points. Review was AI-assisted and has not received an independent second review. Transcription checks do not establish the accuracy of the original instruments.

Tables 4–7 contain depth profiles. This initial delivery deliberately includes only the complete 0.0 m surface row. Unreviewed cells, blank cells at other depths and other tables are excluded. No missing values are replaced with zero.

Reported sampling window: 09:00–10:30; the timezone is not specified. The observations are associated with their sampling dates in 2000, not the 2002 defense or the 2016 online publication date.
